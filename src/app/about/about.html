02 — MY STORY

Where it started.

My journey started in Klerksdorp, where I grew up and completed my schooling at Milner High School.

After high school, I decided to pursue my interest in technology by studying Computer Systems Engineering at Tshwane University of Technology , which gave me a strong foundation in computing, systems, programming and problem solving.

Over time, my interest moved deeper into software development. I began exploring how applications are designed, how frontend and backend systems communicate, and how technology can be used to solve everyday problems.

Today, I enjoy working across the full development process — from creating interfaces and writing APIs to testing, improving and deploying applications.
